Documentation migration

A clear path from the proposal to the work.

Keep the three existing documentation tabs. Give every page one clear job, connect related content, and migrate in small, verifiable batches.

This handbook brings together the proposal, architecture, evidence, page decisions, and delivery progress. Start with the approach, then explore the implementation detail when you need it.

Recommended reading order

1. The meeting brief →The recommendation, what changes, and the decisions to make.2. Understand Diátaxis →Tutorials, how-to guides, reference, and explanations.3. Review the architecture →How the three tabs work together and where information belongs.4. Explore individual pages →Every existing-page decision and every proposed new page.5. Plan the rollout →The Diamond pilot, migration batches, and prerequisites.6. Track delivery →Status, ownership, notes, and implementation links in one place.
